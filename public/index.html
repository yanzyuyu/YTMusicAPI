Available API Endpoints
GET /api/search?q={query}&type={songs|videos|albums|artists|playlists}
Search tracks on YouTube Music with title, artist, album, duration, and thumbnail metadata.
GET /api/song?id={videoId}
Get song details, duration in seconds, views, and embed playback URLs.
GET /api/next?id={videoId}
Retrieve up to 50 recommended tracks in the radio queue and watch next playlist.
GET /api/lyrics?id={videoId}&title={title}&artist={artist}
Fetch plain text and timestamped synced lyrics (LRCLIB fallback).
GET /api/stream?id={videoId}&play={true|false}
Get direct audio stream streams or redirect 302 directly to high bitrate audio stream.
GET /api/playlist?id={playlistId}
Extract playlist metadata, track list, and author info.
GET /api/health
Check system health, engine status, and server uptime.
